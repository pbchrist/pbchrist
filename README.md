---

### What's here

**[talent-market-map](https://github.com/pbchrist/talent-market-map)** — A job
description in, the non-obvious talent pools out. Names the capability under the
job title, then finds the industries where people build that capability without
ever holding that title, with the transfer logic and pasteable search strings.
Holds no candidate data. Scrapes nothing.

**[narrative-sourcing](https://github.com/pbchrist/narrative-sourcing)** — Reads
a career as a story rather than a keyword list: what someone left, what they're
reaching for, what's unresolved. Every inference must quote the candidate
verbatim or it gets deleted. Produces a brief for a human to write from — there
is no message field, and a test enforces that.

**[narrative_heuristics](https://github.com/pbchrist/narrative_heuristics)** —
AI personas that invent their own analysis rules from an abstract personality
model, then run those rules against real data.

---

### The thesis

Every sourcing tool searches the same pool and writes the same outreach.
Coverage is solved; response rates are falling. What still matters is knowing
where else to look — and then saying something true to the person you find.

So my tools do the first part and refuse to do the second. AI is good at finding
people and bad at writing to them. The industry has that backwards.
